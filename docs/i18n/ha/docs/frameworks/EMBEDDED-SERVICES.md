ta amfani da maɓallan API), ayyukan da aka haɗa suna aiki a na'ura ɗaya da OmniRoute kuma suna sadarwa ta loopback.

## 1. Bayani

### Me ya sa ake amfani da ayyukan da aka haɗa?

An haɗa ayyuka guda biyar:

| Aiki            | Kunshin npm                                   | Tashar tsoho | Manufa                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------- | :----------: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **9Router**     | `9router`                                     |    20130     | Na'urar tura AI da OmniRoute zai iya amfani da ita a matsayin ƙaramin mai samar da sabis. Ana gabatar da samfura a matsayin `9router/{sub}/{model}`                                                   |
| **CLIProxyAPI** | Fayil ɗin binary na sakin GitHub (`cliproxy`) |     8317     | Adaftar proxy ta cikin gida don hanyoyin tabbatar da izini na Anthropic CLI. Tana samar da madadin tura zirga-zirga idan wa'adin token ɗin OAuth ya ƙare                                              |
| **Mux**         | `mux` (`mux server` marar UI)                 |     8322     | Daemon na cikin gida don tsara aikin wakilai (coder/mux). Ana sarrafa zagayowar rayuwarsa kawai — ba wurin tura zirga-zirga ba ne (babu proxy na LLM).                                                |
| **Bifrost**     | `@maximhq/bifrost`                            |     8080     | Backend na relay na ƙofar AI da aka gina da Go. Lokacin da yake aiki, hanyar relay (`/v1/relay/`) tana zaɓarsa ta atomatik                                                                            |
| **Dario**       | `@askalf/dario`                               |     3456     | Proxy na biyan kuɗin Claude — madadi/mafaka ga CLIProxyAPI don zirga-zirga mai tsarin Claude-Code; maɓallin da aka saka ya zama `DARIO_ADMIN_TOKEN` wanda ke tsare tsarin sarrafa OAuth na `/admin/*` |

Dukkan biyar suna bin tsarin sa ido iri ɗaya:

- OmniRoute yana girka su a ƙarƙashin `DATA_DIR/services/{name}/` (a ware daga `package.json` na OmniRoute)
- OmniRoute yana ƙaddamar da su kuma yana sa ido a kansu a matsayin matakai na reshe
- OmniRoute yana saka maɓallin API na wucin gadi a cikin muhallin matakin reshen kuma yana sabunta shi ba tare da katse aiki ba (inda hakan ya dace)
- Duk hanyoyin gudanarwa (`/api/services/*`) **LOCAL_ONLY** ne — ana iya samun damar su daga loopback kawai (ƙa'ida mai tsauri #17)

### Muhimman shawarwari (daga tsarin ƙira)

| Shawara                                           | Ƙima                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Samun damar dashboard zuwa UI na asali na 9Router | Reverse proxy a `/dashboard/providers/services/9router/embed/*`                                     |
| Hanyar girkawa                                    | `npm install {package}` ta hanyar `execFile` (babu shell interpolation)                             |
| Yanayin amfani                                    | An yi rajistar mai samar da sabis a matsayin `9router/{sub}/{model}` a cikin injin tura zirga-zirga |
| Gudanar da maɓallin API                           | OmniRoute yana ƙirƙira, yana ɓoye shi lokacin ajiya (AES-256-GCM), sannan yana saka shi ta env      |
| Wurin dashboard                                   | `/dashboard/providers/services` (shafuka uku)                                                       |
| Farawa ta atomatik                                | Maɓallin kunnawa/kashewa ga kowane aiki, a kashe ta tsohuwa                                         |

## 3. Injin yanayin zagayowar rayuwa

```
                    install()
  ┌─────────────┐ ──────────► ┌─────────────┐
  │ not_installed│             │   stopped   │◄──────────────────┐
  └─────────────┘             └──────┬──────┘                   │
                                     │ start()                   │
                                     ▼                           │ stop()
                               ┌──────────┐                      │
                               │ starting │                      │
                               └────┬─────┘                     │
             binciken lafiya ya yi  │         faɗuwa / SIGTERM  │
                               ┌────▼─────┐  (fita cikin 5s)    │
                               │ running  │──── faɗuwa ─────────►┤
                               └────┬─────┘                   ┌─▼────┐
                             stop() │                          │error │
                                    ▼                          └──────┘
                               ┌──────────┐
                               │ stopping │
                               └──────────┘
```

Ana adana yanayi a cikin teburin DB na `version_manager` (ginshiƙin `status`) kuma ana
kwafinsu a yanayin cikin ƙwaƙwalwa na `ServiceSupervisor`. Yanayin cikin ƙwaƙwalwa shi ne
abin dogaro ga tsari mai gudana; yanayin DB shi ne madadin dindindin lokacin farawa.

### Sauye-sauyen yanayi

| Daga            | Lamari                                | Zuwa                      |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| `not_installed` | `install()` ya yi nasara              | `stopped`                 |
| `stopped`       | an kira `start()`                     | `starting`                |
| `starting`      | binciken lafiya ya dawo da 200        | `running`                 |
| `starting`      | tsari ya fita kafin ya zama lafiyayye | `error`                   |
| `running`       | an kira `stop()`                      | `stopping` → `stopped`    |
| `running`       | tsari ya fita ba zato (< 5 s)         | `error` (faɗuwa da sauri) |
| `running`       | tsari ya fita ba zato (> 5 s)         | `error`                   |
| `error`         | an kira `start()`                     | `starting`                |
| kowane          | `stop()` yayin `stopping`             | babu aiki                 |

### Kulle aiki

`ServiceSupervisor` yana jera ayyukan zagayowar rayuwa ta hanyar kullen aiki na async
(`withLock()`). Kiran `start()` a lokaci guda a kan supervisor ɗaya yana haifar da spawn
guda ɗaya tak; mai kira na biyu yana jira sannan ya dawo da matsayin da yake akwai. Wannan
yana hana yanayin tseren aiki idan, misali, farawa ta atomatik da maɓallin UI suka kunna a lokaci guda.

## 4. Bayanin API

Duk hanyoyin da ke ƙarƙashin `/api/services/` **LOCAL_ONLY** ne (loopback kawai, ƙa'ida mai tsauri #17).
Buƙatun da ba na loopback ba suna karɓar `403 LOCAL_ONLY` ba tare da la'akari da auth token ba.

### 4.1 Ƙarshen-hanyoyin 9Router (hanyoyi 11)

#### `POST /api/services/9router/install`

Shigar da 9Router daga npm. Yana ƙirƙirar `DATA_DIR/services/9router/` tare da nasa
`package.json` da `node_modules/`. Ba ya cin karo da dependencies na OmniRoute.

**Jikin buƙata** (duk na zaɓi ne):

```json
{ "version": "latest" }
```

| Fili      | Nau'i    | Tsoho      | Bayani                                    |
| --------- | -------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| `version` | `string` | `"latest"` | alamar sigar npm ko semver da za a shigar |

**Amsoshi:**

| Matsayi | Bayani                                                                    |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `200`   | `{ ok: true, installedVersion: "x.y.z", path: "..." }`                    |
| `400`   | Jikin buƙata ba daidai ba (gazawar tantancewar Zod)                       |
| `409`   | Ana kan shigarwa (an riƙe kulle)                                          |
| `500`   | npm install ya gaza — duba `message` don saƙon kuskure mai sauƙin fahimta |

**Bayanan kula:** Yana amfani da `execFile('npm', [...])` — babu shell, babu interpolation (ƙa'ida mai tsauri #13).
Ana nuna kurakuran EACCES a matsayin saƙonni masu sauƙin fahimta.

---

#### `POST /api/services/9router/start`

Fara 9Router. Yana yi wa supervisor rajista idan ba a riga an yi masa rajista ba, sannan ya kira
`supervisor.start()`. Yana da idempotent idan yana gudana tuni.

**Jikin buƙata:** babu

**Amsoshi:**

| Matsayi | Bayani                                                |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `200`   | Abin `ServiceStatus` (duba schema a ƙasa)             |
| `409`   | Ba a shigar da 9Router ba (`status: "not_installed"`) |
| `503`   | Farawa ya gaza (kuskuren tsari — duba `lastError`)    |

**Schema na ServiceStatus:**

```json
{
  "tool": "9router",
  "state": "running",
  "pid": 12345,
  "port": 20130,
  "health": "healthy",
  "startedAt": "2026-05-25T10:00:00.000Z",
  "lastError": null
}
```

---

#### `POST /api/services/9router/stop`

Tsayar da 9Router cikin tsari. Yana aika SIGTERM, ya jira 15 s, sannan ya aika SIGKILL idan har yanzu yana aiki.
Yana da idempotent idan an riga an tsayar da shi.

**Jikin buƙata:** babu

**Amsoshi:**

| Matsayi | Bayani                             |
| ------- | ---------------------------------- |
| `200`   | `ServiceStatus` (state: "stopped") |
| `503`   | Tsayarwa ta gaza ba zato           |

---

#### `POST /api/services/9router/restart`

Daidai yake da `stop()` sannan `start()` a ƙarƙashin kullen aiki.

**Jikin buƙata:** babu

**Amsoshi:** iri ɗaya da `start` (yana dawo da `ServiceStatus` na ƙarshe).

---

#### `POST /api/services/9router/update`

Yana sabunta 9Router zuwa sabuwar sigar npm. Idan sabis ɗin yana gudana, za a fara
tsayar da shi, a gudanar da npm install (ana shigar da sabuwar sigar a wuri ɗaya), sannan a
sake fara sabis ɗin.

**Jikin buƙata** (duk na zaɓi ne):

```json
{ "version": "latest" }
```

**Amsoshi:**

| Matsayi | Bayani                                                          |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `200`   | `{ ok: true, previousVersion: "...", installedVersion: "..." }` |
| `400`   | Jiki ba daidai ba                                               |
| `500`   | Sabuntawar npm ta gaza                                          |

---

#### `POST /api/services/9router/rotate-key`

Yana samar da sabon maɓallin API na 9Router, yana ɓoye shi yayin ajiya, sannan yana sake kunna sabis ɗin
(idan yana aiki) domin ya ɗauki sabon maɓallin daga muhallinsa. Ana soke tsohon maɓallin
nan take.

**Jikin buƙata:** babu

**Amsoshi:**

| Matsayi | Bayani                                     |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `200`   | `{ keyRotated: true, restarted: boolean }` |

Dashboard ɗin yana saka 9Router web UI a cikin iframe ta hanyar internal reverse
proxy a:

- Yana tura request ɗin zuwa `http://127.0.0.1:{port}/{path}` (loopback kawai)

- Yana sake rubuta HTML responses don saka `<base href>` da daidaita absolute paths (`/foo` → `/dashboard/.../embed/foo`)

WebSocket upgrades na embedded dashboard ana sarrafa su ne ta companion server a wani
dedicated port (duba `src/lib/services/embedWsProxy.ts`).

**Tsaro:** An rarraba embed proxy routes ɗin a ƙarƙashin `LOCAL_ONLY_API_PREFIXES`
kuma ana iya isa gare su daga loopback kawai. Mahari da ya samu JWT ta hanyar
Cloudflare/Ngrok tunnel ba zai iya yin proxy zuwa embedded services ba.

## 5. Tsaro

### Tilasta LOCAL_ONLY (ƙa'ida mai tsauri #17)

Dukkan hanyoyin da ke ƙarƙashin `/api/services/` da `/dashboard/providers/services/*/embed/`
an rarraba su a matsayin LOCAL_ONLY a cikin `src/server/authz/routeGuard.ts`. Binciken loopback
yana gudana ba tare da wani sharadi ba kafin kowane reshen tantancewa:

```
buƙata ta iso
  → isLocalOnlyPath(path)?
      → ba loopback ba → 403 LOCAL_ONLY (koyaushe, kafin binciken tantancewa)
      → loopback       → ci gaba zuwa tantancewa ta yau da kullum
```

Wannan yana hana JWT da ya fallasa (misali, ta hanyar tunnel) jawo `npm install` ko
ƙaddamar da process. Duba `docs/security/ROUTE_GUARD_TIERS.md` don cikakken jadawalin
matakai.

### Shigar da maɓallin API

9Router da Mux suna buƙatar maɓallin API/bearer token don nasu HTTP endpoints.
OmniRoute:

1. Yana samar da maɓalli ta hanyar `crypto.randomBytes(32).toString("base64url")` tare da
   prefix na musamman ga service (`nr_` don 9Router, `mx_` don Mux).
2. Yana ɓoye shi yayin adanawa ta amfani da AES-256-GCM (cipher ɗin da ake amfani da shi don provider credentials).
3. Yana warware ɓoyewar kuma ya shigar da shi a matsayin environment variable lokacin spawn —
   `NINEROUTER_API_KEY` don 9Router, `MUX_SERVER_AUTH_TOKEN` don Mux (ba a taɓa amfani da CLI
   flag ba, don kada token ɗin ya bayyana a cikin `ps`/jerin process).
4. Ba ya taɓa mayar da maɓallin plaintext a cikin kowace HTTP response.

CLIProxyAPI yana karɓar keɓaɓɓen maɓallin data-plane da ake shigarwa lokacin spawn
(`needsApiKey: true` — ana amfani da shi don daidaita model da adapter).

### Kariyar SSRF

Reverse HTTP proxy (`/dashboard/.../embed/[...path]`) an kayyade shi kai tsaye don ya tura
zuwa `http://127.0.0.1:{port}` kawai. Ba ya taɓa bin redirects zuwa wuraren da ba loopback
ba. Ana amfani da library na `ssrf-req-filter` don ƙin duk wani upstream URL da
ke resolve zuwa wajen kewayon loopback.

### Tsaron shell (ƙa'ida mai tsauri #13)

Ana kiran `npm install` ta hanyar `execFile('npm', ['install', pkg, '--prefix', dir])` —
babu template literals, babu shell, kuma babu saka external paths a cikin command
string ta hanyar interpolation. Ana miƙa runtime values (ports, API keys) ta hanyar
`env` object na child.

### Tsabtace kurakurai (ƙa'ida mai tsauri #12)

Dukkan error responses daga `/api/services/*` suna wucewa ta `buildErrorBody()` ko
`sanitizeErrorMessage()`. Ba a taɓa mayar da ɗanyen `err.stack` da `err.message`
kai tsaye ga mai kira.

## 7. Warware Matsaloli

### Sabis ba ya farawa

**Alamomi:** Maɓallin farawa yana mayar da 503, yanayin yana ci gaba da kasancewa `"error"` ko `"starting"`.

**Jerin dubawa:**

1. Duba `GET /api/services/{name}/logs` (ko sashen Logs a cikin dashboard). Nemo
   layuka kamar `Error: ENOENT`, `address already in use`, ko `Cannot find module`.
2. Tabbatar cewa `npm` yana cikin PATH: gudanar da `which npm` daga asusun mai amfani ɗaya da yake gudanar da OmniRoute.
3. Tabbatar cewa an shigar da sabis ɗin: duba `GET /api/services/{name}/status` don
   `installedVersion`. Idan `null` ne, fara da gudanar da shigarwa.
4. Duba cewa `DATA_DIR/services/{name}/node_modules/` yana nan kuma ba fanko ba ne.
5. Duba filin `lastError` a cikin martanin matsayin don gano dalilin fita da aka tsabtace.

---

### Farawa daga sanyi yana jinkiri (> 10 s kafin kaiwa `running`)

**Alamomi:** Yanayin yana ci gaba da kasancewa `"starting"` na dogon lokaci kafin ya koma `"running"` ko `"error"`.

**Bayani:** Farawar 9Router daga sanyi ya haɗa da shigo da manyan bishiyoyin dogaro (DNS,
tunnel, MITM modules). Tsohon tazarar duba lafiya ita ce 2 s tare da yunƙuri 3 kafin
supervisor ya ayyana ƙarewar lokaci (amma yana ci gaba da bincikawa).

**Gyara:** Ana iya saita `healthIntervalMs` da lokacin ƙarewar `waitForHealthy`
(`healthIntervalMs * 3`) a cikin `bootstrap.ts`. Don sabis-sabis masu ɗaukar tsawon
lokaci kafin farawa, ƙara `healthIntervalMs` zuwa 5000 da `stopTimeoutMs` zuwa 30 000.

---

### Rikicin tashar sadarwa (`EADDRINUSE`)

**Alamomi:** Logs suna nuna `address already in use :::20130`.

**Dalilai:**

- Wani tsari yana amfani da port 20130 tuni.
- Ba a dakatar da wani tsohon tsarin 9Router gaba ɗaya ba (zombie PID).

**Gyara:**

1. Canza tsohon port ta amfani da environment variable `NINEROUTER_PORT` a cikin `.env`.
2. Nemo kuma kashe tsarin da yake haifar da rikici: `lsof -ti :20130 | xargs kill -9`.
3. Ana iya saita port ga kowane sabis a cikin `bootstrap.ts` ta filin `port`.

**Lura:** 9Router yana amfani da port 20130 a matsayin tsoho musamman don kauce wa
rikici da tsohon port 20128 na OmniRoute.

---

### An hana izini (EACCES) yayin shigarwa

**Alamomi:** Shigarwa yana mayar da 500, logs suna nuna `EACCES` ko `permission denied`.

**Dalilai:**

- Tsarin OmniRoute ba shi da izinin rubutawa a `DATA_DIR` ko kundin da yake ɗauke da shi.
- Ana gudanarwa a cikin Docker rootless ba tare da izinin rubutawa zuwa volume ɗin da aka haɗa ba.

**Gyara:**

1. Duba `DATA_DIR` (tsoho: `~/.omniroute/`): `ls -la ~/.omniroute/`
2. Tabbatar cewa mai amfani da ke gudanar da tsarin OmniRoute ne ya mallaki kundin: `chown -R $USER ~/.omniroute/`
3. A cikin Docker, tabbatar cewa volume mount yana da izini da suka dace da mai amfani na container.

---

### Sabuntawa ya gaza (ƙarewar lokacin `npm install` ko kuskuren hanyar sadarwa)

**Alamomi:** Sabuntawa yana mayar da 500 tare da `InstallError`, logs suna nuna ƙarewar lokacin hanyar sadarwa.

**Jerin dubawa:**

1. Tabbatar ana iya isa ga npm registry: `npm ping`.
2. Duba ko akwai corporate proxy: `npm config get proxy`, `npm config get https-proxy`.
3. Gwada shigarwar da hannu: `npm install {package}@latest --prefix ~/.omniroute/services/{name}/`.
4. Idan ana bayan air-gap, sauke tarball tun da wuri sannan a yi amfani da `npm install /path/to/tarball.tgz`.

---

### Sabis yana nuna yanayin `"error"` nan take bayan farawa (rushewa da sauri)

**Alamomi:** Yanayin yana sauyawa daga `"starting"` zuwa `"error"` cikin ƙasa da daƙiƙa 5.
`lastError` yana nuna `"Fast crash (exited with code 1)"`.

**Jerin dubawa:**

1. Karanta cikakken ƙarshen log: `GET /api/services/{name}/logs?tail=500`.
2. Dalili na yau da kullum: rashin environment variables da sabis ɗin yake buƙata.
3. Don 9Router: tabbatar cewa `NINEROUTER_DISABLE_MITM=true` da
   `NINEROUTER_DISABLE_TUNNEL=true` suna cikin env da aka miƙa lokacin spawn (duba
   `installers/ninerouter.ts` `resolveSpawnArgs`).

## 8. Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

**T: Zan iya buɗe ƙarshen sabis-sabis ɗin da aka gina a ciki ga abokan hulɗar da ba na loopback ba?**

A'a. Matakin LOCAL_ONLY an tsara shi da gangan (doka mai tsauri #17). Hanyoyin da za su iya kiran
`npm install` ko ƙaddamar da tsarin `node` dole ne kada zirga-zirgar da ba ta loopback ba
ta iya isa gare su, domin idan JWT ya fallasa ta hanyar tunnel (Cloudflare, Ngrok, Tailscale),
in ba haka ba zai ba da damar ƙaddamar da kowane irin tsari. Babu keɓancewar ficewa ga
`/api/services/` — sabanin `/api/mcp/`, an cire shi daga jerin tsallake binciken manage-scope.
Duba `docs/security/ROUTE_GUARD_TIERS.md`.

---

**T: Shin 9Router da CLIProxyAPI za su kasance samammu a turawar production/cloud?**

Eh. Dukkan sabis ɗin biyu suna bin tsarin fifita-gida iri ɗaya da OmniRoute kansa. Suna aiki
a kan na'ura ɗaya kuma suna sadarwa ta loopback. "Production" a nan yana nufin VPS
ko sabar gida inda aka tura OmniRoute, ba wani mai samar da cloud na nesa ba.

---

**T: Ta yaya zan bincika matsalar supervisor?**

1. Bi rafin log na SSE kai tsaye: `curl -N http://localhost:20128/api/services/9router/logs`.
2. Duba tsararrun logs a cikin fitarwar pino ta OmniRoute da aka tace ta namespace na
   `service:supervisor`.
3. Duba layin DB: `sqlite3 ~/.omniroute/omniroute.db "SELECT * FROM version_manager WHERE tool='9router'"`.
4. Yi amfani da `GET /api/services/9router/status` don ganin yanayin aiki na yanzu, PID, lafiyar sabis,
   da `lastError` a kira guda.

---

**T: Supervisor yana nuna `health: "degraded"` ko `health: "unknown"` amma state ɗin shi ne `"running"`. Wannan matsala ce?**

`"degraded"` yana nufin gwajin lafiya ya dawo da amsar da ba 200 ba. `"unknown"` yana nufin babu
wani gwaji da ya kammala tukuna (tseren lokaci da binciken farko). Dukkansu na wucin gadi ne yayin farawa.
Idan health ya ci gaba da kasancewa `"degraded"` fiye da ms `healthIntervalMs * 3` bayan
`"running"`, sabis ɗin da aka gina a ciki yana aiki amma HTTP API ɗinsa ba ya amsawa. Duba
ko port ɗin da ke cikin amsar status daidai ne da kuma ko sabis ɗin yana sauraro
a wannan port ɗin da gaske.

---

**T: Zan iya canza maɓallin API na 9Router ba tare da cikakken sake farawa ba?**

A'a. Ana miƙa maɓallin API ga 9Router ta hanyar environment variable a lokacin ƙaddamarwa.
Ba za a iya canza environment variables a cikin tsari mai gudana ba. `POST .../rotate-key`
yana dakatarwa sannan ya sake kunna sabis ɗin ta atomatik don amfani da sabon maɓallin. Juyawar maɓallin
za ta fara aiki cikin `stopTimeoutMs` na sabis ɗin (tsoho 15 s) tare da lokacin
farawarsa.

---

**T: Mene ne iyakar ring buffer kuma me ke faruwa idan ya cika?**

Kowane sabis yana da keɓaɓɓen ring buffer na 5 MB. Idan buffer ɗin ya cika, ana cire
tsofaffin layukan log don samar da wuri ga sababbi. Event na SSE `snapshot` yana dawo da
layuka mafi sabo a cikin iyakar `tail`. Ba a adana logs a faifai sai idan
an saita `logsBufferPath` a layin DB.
